Pico VR tests

Each page is a small room. For each one, please:

  1. Enter VR and stay in for at least 5 seconds.
  2. Look around, move with the left stick and turn with the right stick.
  3. Exit VR. The page shows PASS or FAIL at the top, with a log underneath.
  4. Tell us which pages failed. For a failed page, a screenshot of the log is the most useful thing you can send.

Series A: one change at a time

Each page adds one thing to the page before it. The first page that fails points to the cause.

    Series B: one feature on its own

    Each page is the baseline plus one feature. Only needed if series A fails somewhere.
